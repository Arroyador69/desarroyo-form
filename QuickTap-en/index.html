Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Have questions? Here we answer the most common ones about the app, privacy, languages, currencies, and business model.
Is QuickTap free?

Yes. You get 7 days with all features. After that, you can continue with a one-time lifetime payment that unlocks Premium, backups, and synchronization.

Do I need internet to use the app?

No. Everything works offline. You'll only need a connection to register, activate Premium, or sync your backup.

How secure is my data?

Data lives on your device. Premium backups are encrypted with AES-256 and only you can decrypt them.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes, by activating Premium you can restore your backup on any iOS or Android device and keep them synchronized.

What currencies and languages does it support?

More than 15 currencies (USD, EUR, MXN, ARS, COP, etc.) and 12 languages with automatic detection.
