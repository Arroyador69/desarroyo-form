Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Have questions? Here we answer the most common ones about the app, privacy, features, and business model.
Is Planova Studio free?

Yes. You get 7 days with all features. After that, you can continue with a one-time lifetime payment that unlocks Premium, cloud sync, and all advanced features.

Do I need internet to use the app?

No. Everything works offline. You'll only need a connection to register, activate Premium, or sync your content.

How secure is my content?

Content is stored locally on your device. Premium cloud sync uses AES-256 encryption and only you can access it.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes, by activating Premium you can sync your content across all your devices and keep them synchronized.

What platforms does it support?

Planova Studio supports planning for Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, and more. You can organize content for any platform.
