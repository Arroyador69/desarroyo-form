Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Have questions? Here we answer the most common ones about the app, privacy, features, and business model.
Is Novawidget Pro free?

NovaWidget Pro is available with a one-time lifetime payment that unlocks all advanced features.

Do I need internet to use the app?

No. Everything works offline. You'll only need a connection to register, activate Premium, or sync your widgets.

How secure are my widgets?

Widget configurations are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage systems. Your data never leaves your device.

Does NovaWidget Pro collect any data?

No. NovaWidget Pro does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data. Everything operates entirely on your device.

What iOS versions does it support?

NovaWidget Pro requires iOS 16.0 or later to use widget customization features.
