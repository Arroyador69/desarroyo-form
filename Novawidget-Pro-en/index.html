Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Have questions? Here we answer the most common ones about the app, privacy, features, and business model.
Is Novawidget Pro free?

Yes. You get 7 days with all features. After that, you can continue with a one-time lifetime payment that unlocks Premium, cloud sync, and all advanced features.

Do I need internet to use the app?

No. Everything works offline. You'll only need a connection to register, activate Premium, or sync your widgets.

How secure are my widgets?

Widget configurations are stored locally on your device. Premium cloud sync uses AES-256 encryption and only you can access them.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes, by activating Premium you can sync your widgets across all your iOS devices and keep them synchronized.

What iOS versions does it support?

NovaWidget Pro requires iOS 16.0 or later to use widget customization features.
