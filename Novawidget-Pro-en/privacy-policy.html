NovaWidget Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 17, 2025

NovaWidget Pro respects your privacy.

This application is designed to operate entirely on your device.

Data Collection

NovaWidget Pro does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data.

We do not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Contact details
  • Location data
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Identifiers
  • Tracking information

Local Data Storage

All content created within the app (such as notes, widget configurations, and settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage systems.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us.

Third-Party Services

NovaWidget Pro does not integrate third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Payments

All payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

NovaWidget Pro does not receive or store any payment or billing information.

Children's Privacy

NovaWidget Pro does not knowingly collect data from children and is compliant with App Store guidelines for users of all ages.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects user privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly.
